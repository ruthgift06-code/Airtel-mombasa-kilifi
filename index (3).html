Questions people ask before ordering

If yours isn't here, send it on WhatsApp and you'll get a straight answer.

Is the internet really unlimited?

Yes. The monthly packages have no data cap, so you can stream, work and game without counting bundles. During very busy hours speeds can vary depending on how far you are from the 5G mast.

Do I need a phone line, cable or trenching?

No. The router connects over the 5G network, so nothing is dug up and no cable is pulled to your house. You just need power and to be inside a covered area.

How much does installation cost?

Delivery to your home is free. You pay a one-time setup fee for the router and WiFi equipment, plus a separate Ksh 1,000 installation fee — both shown next to each package above. There's nothing extra after that.

How long does delivery take?

Orders confirmed before 4pm are usually delivered the same day within Mombasa Island, Nyali, Bamburi and Mtwapa. Kilifi Town, Malindi and other areas further out are next day.

Can I move the router to another house?

Yes. The router is portable — carry it to your new home, plug it in, and as long as the area has 5G coverage it will work. Tell us before you move and we'll check the new address for you.

How do I renew each month?

Renew from your phone with M-PESA using the line that came with your router. We send a reminder before your package expires and can renew on your behalf if you prefer.

What if the signal is weak in my house?

Our technician tests several spots during installation and mounts the router where the signal is strongest. If it drops later, call us and we'll come back and reposition it at no cost.
